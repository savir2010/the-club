# The Club: luxury redesign (all routes)

Scope: every route (age gate, /, /join, /signin, /shop, /shop/[id], cart drawer, Nora chat, /checkout, /orders/[id], /account, /account/subscriptions). Redesign replaces the "Members' Desk in Daylight" world and the marketplace storefront. Modes: / is Persuade; shop and product pages are Persuade/Operate; join, checkout, orders and account are Operate. All behavior, agent integration, data and copy stay.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A members' house with a growing estate behind it, presented like Aesop/Byredo/Aman: few things, large, quiet. Refuses the marketplace (espresso bar, stars, badges, rails, raised cents, pill buttons).

OWN-WORLD: Off-white #FAF9F6 and Cream #F3F0E9 grounds, Black #111111 type and actions, Charcoal #242424, Stone #B5AEA3 and Taupe #C9C1B6 for rules, grounds and image plates. Square corners, 1px hairlines, no shadows at rest. Cormorant Garamond 500 display with controlled tracking; Inter 400/500 body; small tracked-caps (0.14em) only for nav and button labels. Product photos sit on one warm plate, 4:5.

STORY: The visitor sees the estate, understands it's a members' delivery club with a concierge, browses a calm shelf, and starts shopping or asks Nora.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed aerial estate video in full, vibrant colour (user: "I want the colors of the video to be shown vibrantly"), fixed, replacing MoltenMetal; only soft top/bottom shade for legibility; transparent header in off-white; headline in Cormorant 64–112px lower-left, subline, black-on-light "Start shopping" and underlined "Sign in".

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "Redesign the entire website to feel like a sophisticated, high-end luxury brand. Take inspiration from Byredo, Aesop, and Aman." with pinned palette, "Didot, Bodoni, or Cormorant Garamond for headlines", "Large cinematic homepage hero" and the user's aerial video ("The video should replace the metal pipes"). Signature interaction: product plate crossfades (420ms) to an authored detail view with a quiet "Discover" line rising; name and price stay fixed.
