# The Club — demo site (all routes)

Scope: age gate, /, /join, /shop (+ product drawer, phone panel), /checkout, /orders/[id], /account, /account/subscriptions. Visual-only pass: no AI backend; the concierge chat shows a frozen Scene 2 state.
Modes: / is Persuade; join, shop, checkout, orders and account are Operate.
Pinned by brief: tokens, Cormorant Garamond + Inter, page structure in the demo brief, imagery rules. Code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private members' desk at dusk, not a dispensary menu. Refuses the storefront grid of product tiles and the SaaS support-chat look; the conversation and the ledger of what you're getting are the store.

OWN-WORLD: Deep green-black canvas #0F1A14, raised #17241C, ivory #F3EEE4 type, sage-muted #A9B3A6, brass #B8925A used about 5%. Hairlines at 12% ivory, 1px; no heavy shadows. Cormorant for display and for every price numeral; Inter for interface. Monogram "C" in a brass hairline ring is the seal. Products are drawn vessel silhouettes on tonal plinths, never photos of cannabis.

STORY: The visitor understands it is a members-only concierge, believes it is discreet and fairly priced, and requests membership; the member talks to Nora, watches the cart fill and approves in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: a full-bleed dusk saguaro photo with the headline set large in Cormorant at the lower left over the dark foreground, the subline beneath, and brass Request membership plus outline Sign in. Monogram top left; Sign in and Request membership top right. Shop: 260px rail | chat | 360px cart, Nora's header across the chat.

FORM: Pinned by brief (brief beats the roll). Signature move: the member ledger: retail struck through with a hairline, member price in brass Cormorant numerals, bundle price beneath, set identically on cards, cart, checkout, subscriptions and the phone. seed:b0d450d529ca8b7d (roll run after build; the brief-pinned direction overrides the roll and stands)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
