# Imagery

The demo uses no photography.

- Landing page: the MoltenMetal WebGL layer in light mode (src/components/LandingMotion.tsx).
- Join page panel and Nora's avatar: the CSS aurora (src/components/Aurora.tsx, styles in globals.css).
- Products: authored SVG placeholders, one per category (src/components/ProductArt.tsx).

## Bank logos

- public/img/banks: six bank logos supplied by the client for the demo bank picker. They are trademarks of their owners. The demo connects to none of these banks; clear their use before anything goes beyond a private demo.

## Products

- data/catalog.json and public/img/products: 29 products (names, brands, descriptions, photos, THC/CBD, sizes, retail prices) from the Sol Flower Shea menu (livewithsol.com), used with permission from the founder of Copperstate Farms. Each entry keeps its source_url. Ratings, review counts, member prices and bundles are demo values.
